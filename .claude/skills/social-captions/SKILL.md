# Social captions that sound human

For Sam at Faith Vision Media (@faithvisionsmedia, shotbyfvm.com), a real estate photo/video business in Buffalo, NY. Captions go on listing videos, business promos and behind-the-scenes posts.

## Before writing
1. If a video or photos are attached, look at them first (pull a few frames). Write about what is actually on screen. Don't invent features you can't see.
2. Find the one thing that stands out: a moment, a detail, the light, or what the shoot was like. One idea per caption.
3. If you don't know a key fact (price, beds/baths, agent handle, whether it's a short-term rental), leave a [bracket] placeholder. Don't guess.
4. If the platform isn't named, write for Instagram and offer the others.

## How it should sound
- Write the way Sam would text a friend about the shoot. First person ("we", "I") when it fits.
- Short. The first line is the hook, because it's all people see before "more".
- Specific and concrete: "the light hit the gate right at 5pm" beats "stunning golden hour views".
- Plain words. Fragments are fine. Lowercase is fine if it fits the post.
- 0–2 emojis, only where they'd naturally go.

## Banned (these make captions sound robotic)
- Realtor clichés: stunning, breathtaking, nestled, oasis, retreat, dream home, luxury living, elevated, curated, sanctuary, "welcome home", "hits different", "where X meets Y".
- Listing a bunch of features in one sentence ("pool, spa, fire pit and sunsets").
- Em dashes, rhetorical-question hooks ("Ever dreamed of…?"), lines that sound like inspirational quotes.
- Groups of three adjectives, every line ending in an emoji.
- "Tag someone who…" and other generic engagement bait.

## Platform notes
- **Instagram**: 1–3 lines. Line 1 hook, line 2 optional detail, line 3 optional casual credit/CTA ("shot for @[agent]", "booking listings in Buffalo, DM me"). Then a blank line, a 📍 location line, and 5–10 hashtags (location, niche, #faithvisionmedia). No hashtags in the body.
- **TikTok**: one line, under ~100 characters, reads like a thought, not a headline. 3–5 hashtags inline at the end.
- **Facebook**: 2–4 plain sentences, written for local people (neighborhood, agent, what's next). 0–2 hashtags. Link to shotbyfvm.com only if it's a business promo.
- **YouTube Shorts**: a title under 60 characters that says what it is, plus a one-line description and 2–3 hashtags.

## Output
Give 3 options per platform with different angles (a moment, a detail, a behind-the-scenes take). Add one short line on which to use and why. Don't explain the rules.

## Self-check before sending
Read each caption out loud in your head. If it sounds like a Zillow description or an ad, rewrite it. If you could post the exact same caption on any other house, it isn't specific enough.
