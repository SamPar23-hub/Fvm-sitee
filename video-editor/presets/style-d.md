# Style D — Cinematic Statement
Reference: instagram.com/reel/DRit44xEUBA (moody car/window film, "Standing still is a choice…")

**Feel:** a short film. Slow, heavy, serious.

- **Letterbox:** footage lives in a wide band in the middle; black above and below.
- **Huge stacked caps** (Archivo Black), 1–4 words building down the frame ("FEEL / IT / IN / YOUR"), allowed to overlap the footage and spill into the black.
- **Small subtitle line** under the band typing out the full sentence (thin white, sentence case). Built in the graphics step.
- **Black text-only beats:** a word alone on black between scenes.
- **Black-and-white section** for the emotional turn (second half).
- **Shots:** slow, handheld, close: hands on the wheel, gear shift, face in window light, walking away.
- **Sound:** deep hits / low booms on big words, ambient drone or piano. No pop SFX.
- **Grade:** dark. Exposure clearly down, contrast up, saturation down. B&W where noted.
