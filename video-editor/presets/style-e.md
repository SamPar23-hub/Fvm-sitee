# Style E — Story Vlog
Reference: instagram.com/reel/C_esfrPuZj2 ("IMAGINE, WAITING 4 YEARS FOR THIS:")

**Feel:** personal story with a payoff. Backstory fast, then breathe.

- **Open on the hook:** dark night b-roll with a title at the top in tight condensed caps ("IMAGINE, WAITING 4 YEARS FOR THIS:"). Pass it with `--title`.
- **Captions:** one word at a time, italic caps (Barlow SemiBold Italic), lower-middle, each word lights up as it's said.
- **Pacing:** first ~15s = rapid backstory b-roll, a new shot every 0.5–1s (car, Airbnb, city, night). Then hold on a wide, full-body tripod shot of the speaker.
- **Picture-in-picture** selfie insert once, for a real moment.
- **Small @handle watermark** with Instagram icon, upper right/left. Use @shotbyfvm (or the personal handle).
- **Sound:** soft music bed, subtle risers into the payoff.
- **Grade:** dark and moody, cool shadows.
