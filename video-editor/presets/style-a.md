# Style A — Talking Head + Cards
Reference: instagram.com/reel/DWe4lKfiZzA (desk educator, "Realtors turn one listing into these 6 Reels")

**Feel:** calm expert at a desk. One continuous take. The info does the work.

- **Hook title** pinned in the middle-upper frame for the first ~15s, white serif (DM Serif Display), 1–2 lines. Pass it with `--title`. Write it as a promise: "Realtors turn one listing into these 6 Reels".
- **Captions:** 2–3 words, bold white sans (Poppins Bold), at chest height under the title. Slight pop-in.
- **B-roll as cards, never full screen.** Small rounded cards floating over the chest:
  - Intro: a 3x2 grid of listing thumbnails that swap every ~0.5s.
  - Each point: one portrait card (about 40% width) showing that example.
- **Section labels** ("No. 1 is", "Reel No. 2") as the caption on its own beat.
- **Cuts:** remove pauses only. No punch-ins. No transitions.
- **Sound:** soft click on each card appearing. Music optional, very low (-28 LUFS under voice).
- **Grade:** natural, exposure down a touch.
- Cards and grids are built in the `graphics` step (HyperFrames or Final Cut titles), using footage from the listing.
