# Style C — Cinematic Voiceover
Reference: instagram.com/reel/DgIf8wWgzRW (lifestyle montage, "you see most agents hire one company…")

**Feel:** voice telling a story over beautiful footage. Fast, rhythmic.

- **Structure:** voiceover (or talking audio) laid over b-roll. Cut b-roll every **1–4s**, on phrase or beat.
- **Captions build word by word**, wrapping onto new lines, then clear at the end of the phrase. Bold white rounded sans (Poppins Bold), center.
- **In-scene stylized text** on one key word (e.g. red graffiti "STYLE" tracked onto the road). Max 1–2 per reel.
- **Color bumper:** one beat on a moving gradient (deep purple → magenta/pink) with captions on it, then fade back to footage.
- **Shots:** POV driving, interiors with light, person at a pool, house exterior with people walking in. For listings: exterior → standout room → detail → lifestyle → exterior wide.
- **Sound:** whooshes on the bumper and on the stylized word. Music bed under voice.
- **Grade:** natural, exposure down a touch, keep skies blue.
