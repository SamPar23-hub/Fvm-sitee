# Style B — Raw Selfie
Reference: instagram.com/reel/DgGGq1YIPb6 (car selfie, "You can't scale a media company off one-off projects")

**Feel:** real, unpolished, one person talking straight to you. The edit stays invisible.

- **Captions:** 3–5 words, white condensed sans (Barlow Condensed SemiBold), soft shadow, just below the face (~64% down). No animation.
- **Cuts:** every pause over ~0.3s. Alternate a 1.0x / 1.08x punch-in on each cut so jumps feel intentional.
- **No graphics, no b-roll, no title.**
- **Sound:** no SFX. Music only if asked.
- **Grade:** natural, exposure down a touch. Keep it looking like an iPhone, not "graded".
- First tested on the owner's 15s mic clip (2026-11-25): 15.5s → 9.4s, 4 segments.
